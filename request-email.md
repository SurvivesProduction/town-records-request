# Request email template

Copy everything below the line into an email to your town assessor (or the town clerk, if there is no assessor's email). Fill in the bracketed parts.

---

Hello [Assessor name],

Under the Connecticut Freedom of Information Act, I'm requesting an electronic copy of the Assessor's CAMA data for all parcels in the Town of [Town] (all property uses, not only residential).

If your office uses Vision, the standard Warren Group extract (residential, commercial and condo) is exactly what I'm after.

Otherwise, ideally as CSV or Excel, one row per building, with these fields where your system has them:

- Parcel: Unique ID, Map/Block/Lot, Location, Property Use, Acres
- Building: Building number, Style, Year Built, Effective Year Built, Living Area, Stories, Exterior Wall (siding material), Roof Cover, Units
- Owner: Owner name and full mailing address

Plus two separate files keyed by the same Unique ID, if possible:

- Sales: Sale Date, Sale Price, Deed Type, Volume/Page
- Permits: Permit Number, Permit Type, Date Issued, Description, and Status, going back as far as the records do

If permit records are kept by the Building Department rather than the Assessor's office, I'd appreciate it if you could forward that part or let me know who to ask.

If there's a fee for the electronic copy, please tell me the amount before processing. I'm also happy to take whatever standard export your software produces if that's easier than a custom one.

Thank you,
[Your name]
